# Design System: Tomas Bitvinskas | Executive Evidence Dossier

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Executive Evidence Dossier & Proof Constellation"**

This portfolio presents Tomas Bitvinskas's applied AI engineering capabilities to recruiters, technical leads, and collaborators through an authoritative, calm, and highly scannable single-column dossier with an interactive Proof Constellation SVG relationship network.

## Key Principles

1. **Recruiter-First Hierarchy**: Identity, AI automation positioning, and direct contact actions are immediately accessible in the first viewport.
2. **Interactive Proof Constellation**: The single extraordinary interactive moment connects commercial practice, signature thesis research, production projects, and core capabilities with visible path illumination and smooth in-page target scrolling.
3. **Structured Chapter Architecture**: Divided into 4 clear chapters (Commercial Experience @ NNCODE, Signature Research & Thesis, Production Systems, Credentials & Technical Stack).
4. **Adaptive Themes & Print Mode**: Native Light, Dark, and System theme toggle with WCAG AA compliance (≥ 4.5:1 text contrast), plus a dedicated `@media print` 2-page black-and-white stylesheet.
5. **Zero-JS Resilience**: Complete English CV content is delivered directly in semantic HTML5.
